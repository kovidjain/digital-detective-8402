# Task 1.3 — Computer systems around you

| Computer-based system | Main purpose | One input | One output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automated teller machine | Provides banking services such as cash withdrawal. | Card and PIN | Cash dispensed |
| Traffic-light controller | Controls the signals at a road intersection. | Vehicle detector or crossing button | Signal changes colour |
| Supermarket self-checkout | Lets a shopper scan and pay for items. | Barcode scanner | Updated total on the screen |

Each system receives information through a reader, sensor, or button. It processes that information with software and produces a physical or on-screen result.
